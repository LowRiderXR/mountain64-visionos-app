# Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Mountain 64 for Apple Vision Pro collects no personal data and sends nothing over the network.

**No tracking, no analytics.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries and does not track you.

**No network access.** The app does not connect to the internet.

**Save data stays on your device.** Your progress and settings are stored only in the app's own storage on your Vision Pro. They are not shared with other apps, and deleting the app deletes them.

**Head tracking is used only for the picture.** The app uses the position of your head to render the 3D view and to place the pause menu. This information is not stored or sent anywhere.

**Crash reports through Apple.** If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, or if you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may pass crash reports, anonymous usage statistics or your feedback on to the developer. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing.

**Builds from source.** Builds made with the Diagnostics configuration write a local performance log inside the app's storage. The builds distributed through TestFlight and the App Store do not.

**Contact.** For questions about privacy, open an issue in this repository on GitHub.
